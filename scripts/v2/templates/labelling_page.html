Disclosure labelling sheet

45 AI-use statements from ICLR 2026–2027, COLM 2026 and ICML 2026, in random order. Your labels are the first human check of the study's classifier, so label what the text says, not what you think the authors did. Answers save as you click.

How to label (one minute)
Claims use
The authors say AI was used for at least one thing, and deny nothing.
Use + denial
Says AI was used for some tasks and not for others ("only for grammar; not for ideas").
Denies all
Says no AI was used at all.
No claim
Only boilerplate, unfilled placeholders, or responsibility wording; no actual statement of use or non-use.
Not a disclosure
The text is not the authors describing their own AI use (for example, AI as the research subject, or extraction junk).
Used for
Tick every task the authors say AI did. Polishing: grammar, wording, readability, translation. Drafting: writing or rewriting text, condensing, generating paragraphs. Coding: code, scripts, figures code. Literature: finding or summarising papers. Other: ideas, proofs, experiment design, data, anything else. Tick Can't tell for "helped with writing" that does not say how.
